Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How quickly will someone respond to my enquiry?

An engineer calls back the same working day (Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM). For an active breakdown, call us directly instead of using the form.

Do you provide a fixed quote?

Yes. After a short call and a site visit we send a written quote covering scope, timeline and safety arrangements, so there are no surprises.

What should I include in my request?

The service you need, your site location, the equipment or system involved, and when you want work to start. The quick-add buttons under the message box help you cover these.

Do you handle emergency call-outs?

Emergency call-outs are available 24/7. Call +91 40 6677 8899 and we will dispatch as soon as possible.

Can you work while the plant is running?

It depends on the job and the safety risk. We plan work around your shutdown windows where needed and confirm this during the site visit.

Is my information kept private?

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and are never shared with third parties.
