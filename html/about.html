Our story

From breakdown calls to full shutdowns

We picked up the jobs other contractors wouldn't take on short notice — a seized gearbox at 2am, a leaking line before a Monday start-up. Word travelled between plant managers before it ever reached a sales brochure.

As the calls got bigger, so did the crew. Welding and fabrication came first, then electrical and automation, then full plant installation. Today the same discipline that got a pump running at 2am runs multi-week shutdowns for clients across Tamil Nadu.

What hasn't changed: one supervisor per job, a fixed price before we start, and a written report when we finish.
