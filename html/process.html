01
Phase 01

Site assessment & scope

An engineer walks the site with you, inspects the equipment or area in question, and confirms access, permits and safety requirements before anything is priced.

  • Free on-site walk-through
  • Photographic condition report
  • Risk and access review
  • Scope agreed in writing
02
Phase 02

Fixed quote & method statement

You receive one fixed price, a written method statement and a realistic schedule — no hourly surprises once the crew is on site.

  • Itemised, fixed-price quote
  • Method statement & risk assessment
  • Materials and lead times confirmed
  • Sign-off before mobilisation
03
Phase 03

Scheduling & mobilization

Crew, equipment and materials are booked around your production calendar — single machines to multi-week shutdowns, including round-the-clock shifts.

  • Crew and equipment booked
  • Materials staged in advance
  • Fits around your shutdown window
  • One point of contact confirmed
04
Phase 04

Execution & quality control

Certified crews carry out the work under a designated supervisor. Every weld, alignment and connection is checked and logged as it happens.

  • Certified, supervised crews
  • Daily progress & safety checks
  • Weld maps and inspection logs
  • Client sign-off at each milestone
05
Phase 05

Testing, certification & handover

Systems are pressure or load tested before you see them. You get certificates, as-built drawings and a short handover walk-through with your operators.

  • Pressure / load testing
  • Test certificates & as-built drawings
  • Operator handover & training
  • Ongoing aftercare on request

Process, in plain terms

What to expect once you get in touch.

An engineer calls back the same working day to scope the job, then books a site visit if one is needed before pricing.

If something is found on site that changes the scope, we stop, document it and agree a revised quote with you before continuing.
